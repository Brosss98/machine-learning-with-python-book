Conventions to keep in mind¶

  • Vectors are columns. A vector x∈Rd\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^dx∈Rd is a d×1d\times 1d×1 column, and x⊤\mathbf{x}^\topx⊤ is a row.

  • Indices start at 1 in the mathematics and at 0 in Python. The first sample is x1\mathbf{x}_1x1​ in the formulas and X[0] in the code. This is a frequent source of off-by-one mistakes.

  • Code is written in monospace. Names such as X, y, and w match the symbols in the text whenever possible.

  • Local notation. A chapter may introduce extra symbols for its own purposes. They are defined where they are first used.
