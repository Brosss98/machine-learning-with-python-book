## About this book

Machine learning is built on ideas that every student of linear algebra and calculus already knows: vectors, matrices, projections, eigenvalues, gradients and the chain rule. This book shows how those ideas become working algorithms, starting from nearest neighbors and regularized least squares, passing through kernels, clustering and PCA, and arriving at deep networks, transformers and generative models.

Every chapter pairs mathematics with code. Derivations are written out step by step, every method is implemented in Python on real datasets taken from standard libraries, and each chapter closes with exercises of increasing difficulty, both by hand and on the computer. The chapters are Jupyter notebooks, so you can re-run every computation, change it, and see what happens.
